How GroupLY works

1
Mark two reference points on the target at a known distance — e.g. corners of a 1" grid square. Tap to place, drag to align precisely.
2
Select your caliber. The impact circle will be sized to match your actual bullet diameter. Tap each hole and drag the circle until its edge aligns with the hole edge.
3
Calculate — enter your distance to target and hit Calculate for group size in inches and MOA.
