How to measure your group

1
Mark two reference points at a known distance on your target — for example, the edges of a 1" grid square, or two marks exactly 1" apart.
2
Enter the distance between your reference points in inches.
3
Tap each bullet hole to mark your impacts. Pinch to zoom in for precision.
4
Hit Calculate to see group size in inches and MOA.
