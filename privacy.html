App Borely — iOS
The short version

Borely collects nothing. No account. No analytics. No cloud. Every record you create stays exclusively on your device. It is that simple, and it is important to us, because it is important to you.

1. Overview

Tight Bore LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Borely mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding any information in connection with your use of the App.

Our core principle is simple: Borely does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server or third party under any circumstances. All data you enter in the App remains on your device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, receive, or have access to any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information
  • Barrel data, load data, session logs, or any other content you enter into the App
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or analytics data
  • Usage statistics or crash reports
  • Payment information (purchases are processed entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details)

3. Data Storage

All App data — including barrel records, load library entries, session logs, cleaning logs, and CBTO readings — is stored exclusively on your device using your device's local storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to Tight Bore LLC or any third party
  • Is not backed up to any cloud service operated by us

If you use Apple's iCloud Backup feature, your device backup may include local App data as part of your device backup. That backup is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not ours.

4. In-App Purchases

Borely uses Apple's In-App Purchase system for optional paid features. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. Tight Bore LLC does not process, store, or have access to any payment card information or purchase details beyond confirmation that a purchase was completed.

Apple's handling of payment data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection SDKs. We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any information to share.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. Because we do not collect personal information from any users, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the address below — though we have no mechanism by which such information could be received.

7. Data Security

Because all App data is stored locally on your device and we do not receive or transmit user data, the security of your data depends on your device's security settings. We encourage you to use a strong passcode, enable Face ID or Touch ID, and keep your device's operating system up to date.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, there is no data held by Tight Bore LLC to access, correct, export, or delete. All App data is under your direct control on your own device. You can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App from your device.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this document and make the updated policy available at our website. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

We will notify users of material changes through the App or via the App Store update notes.
