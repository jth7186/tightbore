Features

Everything a precision shooter needs.
Nothing they don't.

Built by a competitive precision shooter and FFL manufacturer. Borely isn't a general-purpose shooting log — it's a barrel lifecycle management system.

01 BARREL LIFE

Round Count Tracking

Track cumulative rounds per barrel across every session. Monitor barrel life against expected thresholds for your caliber, and know exactly where you stand in your barrel's lifecycle.

02 SESSIONS

Detailed Session Logging

Log every range session with load data, round counts, velocities, CBTO measurements, and notes. Each session builds the historical record that makes your barrel data meaningful.

03 WEATHER

Automatic Weather Data

Auto-fetch temperature, altitude, wind, and barometric pressure from your location via the NWS API. Density altitude is calculated automatically. No manual entry required.

04 ANALYTICS

Performance Trends

Visualize velocity, ES/SD, and CBTO drift over cumulative round count. Watch the curves that tell you when a barrel is breaking in, when it's in its prime, and when it's falling off.

05 GROUPLY

Photo Group Measurement

Measure group sizes directly from target photos — built right into Borely. Group data flows straight into your session logs and barrel performance curves. No separate app needed.

06 LOADS

Load Library

Build and maintain a library of your loads with full component details. Attach loads to sessions, compare performance across different recipes, and track what works in your rifle.

Integrated

Grouply — built in, not bolted on.

Measure groups from target photos.

Snap a photo of your target, tap your shots, and Borely calculates group size in MOA or MIL. The measurement flows directly into your session log — no re-entry, no separate app, no lost data.

Unlike standalone group measurement apps, Grouply inside Borely connects every group to a barrel, a load, a round count, and the weather conditions that day. That context is what turns a single number into a trend line.
